# Retention review and privacy follow-up

Prior privacy export and case workflow passed all five suites. This milestone is strictly read-only, including the daily SSH command. No deletion API, destructive CLI, scheduled purge or retention-policy switch exists. The report separates expired authentication records, older closed business records, unreleased drafts, released delivery records, holds and aging privacy requests. The illustrative thresholds (7/30/365 days) are operational review windows only, not legal deadlines or permission to erase anything. Categories overlap. Privacy case age is calculated from the original submission, not the latest status update. Private counts are exposed only in the authenticated artist dashboard and authorized SSH logs. No identifying records are printed. A production backup does not by itself authorize deletion.

## Before enabling any cleanup

Owner must approve a record-class schedule with applicable legal/accounting review, a process for legal holds and unresolved disputes, customer request responses, and secure deletion from primary storage and backup expiry. Distinguish short-lived auth/session/limiter records from financial, agreement and order evidence; preserve active credentials, open orders, payment disputes, released artwork and required audit records. Review old references and unused drafts individually, including dependencies from proof approvals. Validate independent recovery before destructive changes. Future cleanup should use separate narrowly scoped commands, bounded batches, dry runs, explicit authorization, audit counts and tests confirming live records remain untouched. No production data or flags changed by this milestone.
